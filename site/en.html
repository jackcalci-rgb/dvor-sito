A picturesque setting in the heart of the Collio

Dvor was born to celebrate local culinary tradition, offering a journey of flavours in a historic and scenic setting without equal. The house is a building of great cultural value, and it weaves the charm of the past into the present.

Every room creates a welcoming, refined atmosphere. Deeply rooted in its land, the venue is a gastronomic destination of excellence, drawing locals and visitors alike who are looking for authenticity and quality.

Cultural heritage record

Dvor occupies a farmhouse recorded in the General Catalogue of Cultural Heritage as “Friulian-type rural architecture”: a group of buildings around a courtyard, with seventeenth-century origins and a rebuilding after 1918.

Complex
A block-shaped house predating the 1900s, a twentieth-century cellar whose regular courtyard front is marked by rectangular windows, and a church linked to a dwelling.
Structure
Perimeter walls of dressed stone, rectangular plan, pitched roof covered with clay tiles. Condition: good.
Uses over time
Formerly a dwelling, a cellar and a church; today a restaurant. The catalogue also lists it as “Trattoria Dvor”.
Source
General Catalogue of Cultural Heritage, architecture record no. 06 00029111 (compiled in 1993, updated in 2020).

Frequently asked questions

Where is Osteria Enoteca Dvor?

In San Floriano del Collio, in the province of Gorizia, at Via Castello 5 (34070), in the heart of the Collio.

What are the opening hours?

Monday, Tuesday and Friday to Sunday from 11:00 to 22:00. Closed on Wednesday and Thursday.

Do I need to book?

Booking is recommended. You can book online at prenota.osteriadvor.com, by phone on +39 0481 090740 or on WhatsApp +39 392 388 3900.

What kind of food does Dvor serve?

Border and Friulian cuisine: traditional dishes reinterpreted in a modern key, made with fresh, quality ingredients, in rooms of stone, wood and exposed beams.

Which wines do you have?

A wide selection of wines from the Italian and Slovenian Collio, plus sparkling wines, other wines and premium amari. The full wine list is online, and you can taste and buy in the enoteca.

Is there a garden? Can I eat outdoors?

Yes, the garden is among chestnut trees and flowering plants, with views over hills and vineyards. In spring and summer there is an outdoor grill, informal, with table service.

Getting here

San Floriano del Collio is about 6 km from Gorizia, on the border with Slovenia.
